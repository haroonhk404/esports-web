// FAQ

Quick Answers

How do I withdraw winnings?

Winnings are auto-distributed to your registered MetaMask wallet within 1 hour of finals. No withdrawal request needed.

Can I substitute a player mid-tournament?

No mid-circuit substitutions allowed. Roster is locked once registration is confirmed on-chain.

What if my squad disbands?

Squad disbandment forfeits entry fee. Remaining members can register with new squad in a future season.

Is there a backup match server?

Yes. Asia secondary server activates automatically if primary fails. No matches re-played due to server issues.
